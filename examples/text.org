* A small Typst exporter
Plain text, *bold*, /italic/, _underlined_, and +crossed out+.
Inline code: ~array[0]~ and =literal `backticks`=.

** Links and literal punctuation
https://typst.app/docs/

[[https://orgmode.org][Org mode]] exports this text without a template.
Punctuation stays literal: # [brackets] {braces} $5 @name.
